# D-04 Directory listings and claims

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-25)

**Decision.** Staff add salons to a public directory (CSV import or one at a time). An owner claims a listing by
entering the ABN (it must match the listing, or be active on the ABR when the listing has none) **and** proving
control: a link sent to the listing's business email (48 hours, single use), or staff approval when there's no
email or they can't reach it. A claimed listing gets the same free period and founding price as a new salon.

**Why.** ABNs are public, so an ABN alone doesn't prove ownership. Matt wanted "enter an ABN and we check, or we
send an email they need to click".

**Data:** only import data we may use (businesses' own published details, the ABR, licensed data) — not scraped
Google Maps or Seek data.
